Method and accuracy

A patch counts only when all of its check points pass. Patches that pass with the logged positions but fail with a 10 cm / 2° position error are marked uncertain and added to the re-fly plan.

On four simulated structures the engine was compared with the simulator's independent ground truth: at most 1.1 % of the patches it called covered were not fully seen. It has not yet been validated on customer field data.

Provenance

Every report records the exact input files, so the result can be reproduced and audited.

Full coverage means every wall area was imaged at the stated quality. It does not mean every defect was found; the images are still assessed by a qualified inspector. The drone animation replays the logged flight at high speed.
